The desert stretches across the horizon. From this perspective, Pepper and Carrot look like two small figures. The land is mostly barren, but a few plants are visible across the dunes.

Carrot holds his paws on his belly, as his empty stomach makes a sound. Pepper wipes the sweat from her forehead as she leans on a large stick and says something to Carrot.

"I'm totally worn out too Carrot... And it's been days since we've eaten anything."

Staring at a plant that looks like a giant radish, Pepper makes her best impression of Cumin.

"To know the edible plants is to know where to find ready-made potions against hunger!"

Sitting on the floor, Pepper and Carrot are both eating these giant radishes, looking hardly impressed by their meal.

"But real potions?... If only she had taught me a single one!"
